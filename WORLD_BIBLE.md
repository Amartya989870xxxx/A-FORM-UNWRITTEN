# A FORM UNWRITTEN: WORLD BIBLE

> *"An immortal soul. An unfinished form. A story yet to be written."*

This document establishes the canonical worldbuilding, character identity, geography, factions, progression pillars, and long-term narrative architecture for **A FORM UNWRITTEN**. All future chapters, quests, artwork, and dialogue must remain faithful to these foundations.

---

## 1. Core Philosophical Theme

An immortal existence awakens without memory, without biological flesh, and without an anchored place in the physical world. Driven at first by the conviction that something essential was taken from him, he journeys across ruined frontiers and living kingdoms to reclaim the missing aspects of his being.

Every Remnant he anchors and every bond he forms reshapes both how he traverses the world and who he is becoming. Across chapters, a central tension deepens:

**What if reclaiming his original state requires surrendering the relationships, memories, and chosen identity he has forged along the way?**

Exploration, environmental puzzle-solving, discovery, character relationships, and consequential choices drive the experience rather than generic combat grinding.

## 2. Absolute Protagonist Canon

### 2.1 An Existence Without a Physical Body
* The protagonist **does not possess a human body**. He is not a mortal wearing enchanted robes, nor is there hidden skin, eyes, hair, or ordinary anatomy beneath his clothing.
* His visible manifestation in the physical world is sustained entirely by an extraordinary **Stabilizing Garment**.
* The garment lends his bodiless presence a coherent humanoid silhouette, allowing him to walk upon stone, touch ancient mechanisms, converse with mortals, and bear physical mass once anchored.

### 2.2 The Stabilizing Garment (Canonical Playable Form)
* **Visual Signature:** Layered charcoal and dark-ash robes (`#23242b` to `#17181e`), a flowing multi-panel mantle with warm parchment-ash inner lining (`#cbc5b7`), cloth-wrapped greaves, an antique gold waist-ring clasp, and delicate kintsugi-like gold seams (`#d8aa4e` / `#f2cd78`).
* **Concealed Head and Visage:** Swept silver-ash cowl and crest folds frame a completely dark, indistinct interior void. No human face, glowing eyes, or exposed skin ever appear.
* **Kinetics:** Because the garment *is* his physical presence, its mantle, hems, and gold seams respond expressively to wind, drifting, jumping, falling, ground impacts, and Remnant stabilization.
* **Intentional Unresolved Mysteries:** Nobody in Chapter One knows who wove the garment, where it originated, why it binds the protagonist, whether it chose him or was placed upon him, whether it is as old as his soul, or whether stabilization is its sole purpose. Future chapters may offer oblique clues and startled reactions from ancient beings, but its true origin must never be casually explained away.

### 2.3 The Astral Essence (Non-Playable Inner Nature)
* Beneath the stabilizing weave lies an unsettled, shifting multicolored astral prism.
* **Strict Presentation Rule:** This astral essence is **never** the normal playable character model. Ordinary villagers, merchants, and Guild clerks see only the charcoal-and-gold clad traveller.
* Only rare higher entities, ancient wardens (such as Vespera), deep Memory Crucibles, or singular threshold events perceive brief refractions of the prismatic essence beneath the garment's gold seams.

### 2.4 Player-Chosen Name
* The protagonist has no fixed external canon name. At the start of a new journey, the player inscribes a name (defaulting to **The Unwritten** if left blank).
* Characters, Guild charters, chronicle entries, and save records address the protagonist by this chosen name.

## 3. Historical Strata & Timeline

History in *A Form Unwritten* is layered across vastly different scales of time. Low-rank frontier curiosities can brush against pre-human strata, while high-rank political emergencies may stem from events only a decade old.

| Era | Approximate Age | Defining Works & Lingering Traces |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Uncounted Dawn** | `1,000,000+ Years` | Pre-continental basalt monoliths, the Broken Ring-Monoliths suspended in the upper atmosphere, and phenomena predating recorded mortal language. |
| **The Age of Harmonic Masonry** | `~3,000 Years` | Construction of the Crucibles of Mass, Wind-Spire resonance networks, Counterweight Vaults, and the Fractured Mirror-Arches linking frontier thresholds. |
| **Dispersion of the Silent Loom** | `~1,000 Years` | A secretive order of astral weavers vanished from the highland sanctuaries, leaving behind sealed reliquaries and metallic gold thread identical to the protagonist's seams. |
| **The Guild Concordat** | `~240 Years` | Founding of the continental Adventurers' Guild in Aurelia, standardizing Rank `G` through `S` charters, relic registries, and regional cartography. |
| **The Verge Sundering** | `~40 Years` | Violent gravitational fractures tore the Shattered Verge away from the Crown Reaches highways, stranding watchposts and collapsing lower aqueducts. |
| **Present Day** | `Current Decade` | Frontier surveyors cautiously re-map outer ruins while Guild archivists debate sealed reports concerning an unnatural black tempest on the far-eastern sea. |

## 4. Continental Geography

The World Atlas tracks **Regional Exploration** separately from **Known World Exploration**. Across the four chartered continents lie **18 major regions** (`TOTAL_KNOWN_WORLD_REGIONS = 18`). Completing 100% of *The Shattered Verge* honestly represents `5.56%` (`1 / 18`) of the Known World.

### 4.1 Aethelgard & The Crown Reaches *(Northwest Continent, 5 Regions)*
Highland kingdoms built atop tiered sky-aqueducts, wind-carved limestone cliffs, and ancient counterweight citadels.
1. **The Shattered Verge (Chapter One Region):** A sundered highland borderland of floating masonry, Twin Wind-Spires, the Shattered Sky-Arch, the Crucible of Mass, and the subterranean Cradle of First Names.
2. **Highcrown Citadel:** Capital of Aethelgard, where terraced districts encircle an ancient dormant bell-tower.
3. **Ashenweld Frontier:** Mist-drenched quarry towns and Guild border posts where travellers first register formal charters after crossing the Mirror-Arch.
4. **Vaelen Sky-Aqueducts:** Miles-long suspended water-bridges carrying river barges between mountain peaks.
5. **The Sunken Reliquary Trench:** A collapsed gorge of pre-kingdom vaults accessible only to grounded delves.

### 4.2 Aurelia of the Ivory Spires *(Northeast Continent, 5 Regions)*
Seat of continental scholarship, astronomical observatories, and the Grand Ledger Hall of the Adventurers' Guild.
1. **Luminar Grand Spire:** Sovereign city-state housing the Guild's High Charter Council and Relic Vaults.
2. **Caelum Sky-Bridges:** High-altitude alabaster causeways spanning perpetual cloud-seas.
3. **The Glass Archive of Oakhaven:** Monastic repository storing three millennia of rubbed stone steles.
4. **Bellweather Downs:** Rolling pastoral plateaus dotted with fallen pre-epoch ring fragments.
5. **The Hollow Astrolabe:** A subterranean celestial engine buried inverted beneath Mount Caelum.

### 4.3 The Solis Aridlands *(Southwest Continent, 4 Regions)*
Sun-forged brass dominions, volcanic glass calderas, and subterranean oasis kingdoms.
1. **Heliopolis of the Brass Sun:** Trade metropolis powered by solar mirrors and molten counterweight lifts.
2. **Obsidian Caldera:** Ringed crater of resonant black glass where echoes replay spoken vows decades later.
3. **Salt-Glass Caravan Route:** Shimmering white flats navigated by wind-sled convoys and Guild escorts.
4. **Vault of the First Anvil:** A 3,000-year foundry where physical Remnants were once measured and caged.

### 4.4 The Sylphic Marches *(Southeast Continent, 4 Regions)*
River-threaded lowlands, bioluminescent root-forests, and half-submerged tidal archives.
1. **Virelia River-Throne:** Canal capital built across twelve converging waterfalls.
2. **Drowned Library of Merrow:** Flooded vaults whose upper bell-chambers open only during low-tide alignments.
3. **The Root-Woven Fen:** Ancient banyan labyrinth tended by surviving kin of the Wardens.
4. **Lantern-Gate Port:** Easternmost harbor city gazing out toward the forbidden tempest horizon.

### 4.5 The Secret Fifth Continent: Uncharted Abyssal Shroud *(Far East, Excluded from Known World %)*
* Beyond Lantern-Gate Port and the eastern seas of Aurelia churns a permanent horizon of black-violet abyssal mist.
* **Cartographic Exclusion:** Imperial and Guild atlases list only four continents. The fifth landmass contributes `0` regions to standard Known World percentages so players cannot deduce its scale from early completion counters.
* **Unlock Condition:** No ordinary vessel, bridge, or teleport arch can pierce the shroud. Reaching it requires uncovering and completing a multi-chapter, multi-continent hidden world quest (*Cartography of the Black Tempest*).

## 5. Major Factions & The Adventurers' Guild

### 5.1 The Adventurers' Guild
* Operates neutral Charter Halls across all four known continents, issuing exploration commissions, verifying newly mapped ruins, and appraising recovered Relics.
* **Rank Structure (`G` to `S`):**
  * **Rank G (Unregistered Wayfarer):** Unproven travellers operating on provisional frontier notice.
  * **Rank F (Chartered Surveyor):** Awarded upon verifying an authentic ruin anomaly or unsealing a recognized relic cache (achievable in Chapter One via *Threads of the Silent Loom*).
  * **Rank E & D (Field Chronicler / Ruin Delver):** Authorized for cross-province expeditions and hazardous subterranean mechanisms.
  * **Rank C & B ( Spire Envoy / High Warden Associate):** Trusted with kingdom-scale mysteries, unstable Remnant vaults, and multi-party charters.
  * **Rank A & S (Continental Arbiter / Sovereign Witness):** Reserved for individuals whose discoveries alter continental history.

### 5.2 The Wardens of the First Names
* Near-immortal stone and root custodians (including **Oren** and **Vespera**) bound by ancient oaths to tend Memory Crucibles and observe what unbound souls choose when offered physical form.

### 5.3 The Silent Loom *(Vanished)*
* An unrecorded fellowship from a millennium ago whose weavers worked with astral-conductive gold filament. Their hidden caches react specifically to the protagonist's stabilizing garment.

### 5.4 The Concord of Ivory Cartographers
* Aurelia's elite geographical ministry. Publicly they sponsor world surveys; privately their senior censors confiscate ship logs mentioning land silhouettes inside the eastern black tempest.

## 6. Progression Pillars: Remnants, Soul Spectrum & The Black Orb

### 6.1 Remnants of Existence (Transformative Tradeoffs)
Each reclaimed Remnant permanently alters how the Stabilizing Garment interacts with the world, granting new physical agency at the cost of an unanchored freedom:
* **Chapter One (Remnant of Weight):** Transitions the protagonist from **Unanchored State** (airborne drift, rising wind-updraft flight) to **Grounded State** (heavy pressure-plate activation, granite monolith pushing, and midair Ground Impact shattering).
* **Planned Future Remnants:** *Remnant of Echo* (harmonic resonance across sealed bells vs. silent stealth passage), *Remnant of Warmth* (kindling dormant braziers and cold mechanisms vs. passing unseen through frost-wards), and *Remnant of Tether* (anchoring phase-lines across chasms).

### 6.2 The Soul Spectrum
Every consequential dialogue choice, Imprint, Remnant, and hidden discovery shifts six internal affinities persisted in `SaveState`:
* **Azure:** Clarity, stillness, and harmonic attunement.
* **Verdant:** Empathy, living bonds, and open becoming.
* **Amber:** Physical mass, endurance, and grounded permanence.
* **Crimson:** Resolve, sacrifice, and unwavering conviction.
* **Violet:** Liminal memory, grief, and ancient remembrance.
* **Silver:** Astral perception, hidden craft, and Silent Loom resonance.

### 6.3 The Legendary Black Orb
* Spoken of only in damaged marginalia and contradictory Warden warnings, a **Black Orb** is a rare, light-drinking sphere completely unlike golden Remnants.
* **Unpredictable, Double-Edged Nature:** Invoking a Black Orb is never a guaranteed power-up. Depending on the bearer's current Soul Spectrum, location, and unwritten history, a Black Orb might:
  1. Awaken a singular abyssal or eclipse affinity unattainable by normal means;
  2. Invert or destabilize existing Soul Spectrum affinities, altering how sanctuaries and NPCs react;
  3. Impose a subtle perceptual distortion (revealing hidden inscriptions while sealing certain orthodox Guild shrines); or
  4. Remain utterly silent, leaving behind only an indelible mark (`blackOrbUsedCount`) noticed by higher beings.
* **Conflicting Myths:** Guild scholars claim Black Orbs are burnt-out Crucible cores; Sylphic mystics claim they are the calcified identities cast off by immortals who chose to forget everything; censored Lantern-Gate sailors whisper they wash ashore only when lightning strikes inside the eastern Abyssal Shroud.

## 7. Companions & Party Philosophy (Chapters Two and Beyond)

While Chapter One (*The Shattered Verge*) is an intimate solitary awakening punctuated by encounters with Oren and Vespera, the wider RPG introduces a curated cast of **story-driven companions**:
* **Authored People, Never Stat Tokens:** Every companion has personal stakes, regional history, moral boundaries, and evolving opinions on what the protagonist is becoming.
* **Exploration & Puzzle Synergy:** Companions assist in environmental puzzles (holding distant harmonic bells, translating censored Guild ciphers, counter-balancing multi-tier aqueduct locks) rather than acting as automated combat pets.
* **Moral Friction & Loyalty:** Choices regarding Remnants, Imprints, and the Black Orb affect companion trust. A companion may challenge the protagonist's decision, offer an unforeseen peaceful solution to a ruin crisis, or part ways if their core convictions are betrayed.
